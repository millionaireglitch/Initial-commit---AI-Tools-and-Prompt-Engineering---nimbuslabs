# Nimbus University Hostel Rules and Guidelines (Mock Campus Record)

**Document Identifier**: `campus://hostel/rules`  
**Effective Academic Year**: 2026–2027  
**Department**: Office of Campus Residence & Student Welfare  

---

### 1. Curfew & Gate Timings
- **Main Campus Gate Closure**: 22:00 (10:00 PM) on weekdays (Sunday to Thursday).
- **Weekend Gate Closure**: 23:00 (11:00 PM) on Fridays and Saturdays.
- **Late Entry Policy**: Any entry after curfew requires prior written authorization from the Resident Warden or an emergency pass issued via the Campus Portal.

### 2. Room Allocations & Inventory
- Room changes are strictly prohibited without formal allocation orders from the Chief Warden.
- Each resident is accountable for the inventory listed upon check-in (study desk, chair, wardrobe, mattress, electrical fixtures).
- Tampering with electrical fittings or using high-power appliances (electric heaters, induction cooktops) is strictly forbidden.

### 3. Quiet Hours & Common Areas
- **Quiet Hours**: 23:00 to 06:00 daily. High-volume music, noisy group discussions, and instrument practice are prohibited during these hours.
- Study lounges and computer labs remain open 24/7 with biometric access.

### 4. Visitor Policy
- Day visitors are permitted in designated lobby areas only between 09:00 and 19:00.
- Overnight visitors are strictly prohibited in student dormitories. Guest rooms can be reserved through the campus portal subject to availability.

### 5. Disciplinary Sanctions
- Violations of curfew, substance policies, or unauthorized occupancy will result in escalating disciplinary hearings, fines, and potential suspension of residential privileges.
